The problem isn't motivation.
It's the map.

The ambition is everywhere, we see it in every lecture hall. What's missing is the map. Three things stand between students and the opportunities made for them:

01
Scattered

Internships, theses, jobs and programmes live across dozens of websites, newsletters and word-of-mouth. There's no single place to look.

02
Too late

Many of the best opportunities have deadlines students only hear about after they've passed, if they hear about them at all.

03
Hard to match

And even when you find something, it's hard to tell what actually fits your interests, your level and your ambitions.

We're not a job board.

We're a matching programme. Everything on this site is here to show you what's possible, the real magic happens when you come talk to us.

01
Explore

Browse internships, programmes and events. This site is here to get you excited about what's possible.

02
Reach out

For partner roles, contact them directly with the concept email we provide. Not sure what suits you? Plan an appointment instead.

03
Get inspired

Not sure what fits? Plan an appointment and we'll point you to programmes and paths from our database built around your ambitions.
