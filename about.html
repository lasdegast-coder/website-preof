About us.

Impact Connect inspires ambitious students to make an impact beside their studies. We curate internships, thesis placements, events and programmes, and inspire you toward the career and academic stimulation that turns what you study into what you do.

Created by students and supported by Utrecht University, we work differently from a job board: this site is here to show you what's possible and get you excited. The real matching happens in a conversation, you tell us what drives you, and we personally introduce you to the partner, programme or thesis that fits.

How it works.
