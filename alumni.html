Ask an alumni.

The quickest way to find out whether a field suits you is to talk to someone already in it. Students who came through Impact Connect are now working in climate policy, consultancy, research, water management and their own ventures, and they've agreed to be approached by the students behind them. Pick who you'd like to speak to, tell us what you want to ask, and we introduce the two of you by mail.

Two ways to reach them.

Not sure who you need yet

If the real question is "what should I even be doing?", start with us instead. Plan a short appointment, we'll walk through what drives you, and we'll work out together who's worth speaking to.

A 30-minute conversation with the team
You know who you're after

Have a look at who's on the list, pick the person whose path lines up with yours, and send them your question. We read it, decide who can help you best, and introduce the two of you by mail.

Takes two minutes · usually answered within three working days

Nobody on the list fits your question?

What people ask about.

Anything, really. These come up most often — click one to start your question with it.

How did you get into this field? Was the master's worth it? What does a normal week look like? How did you write your thesis at a company? Would you do the same again? What did you wish you'd known in year one? Is there room to make actual impact there? How did you end up abroad?

How it works.

01
You pick someone

Go through the list and send your question to the person whose path lines up with yours. Two minutes.

02
We read it

We look at your question and at who has room. Usually that's the person you picked; sometimes we know someone who fits it better.

03
We introduce you

One mail goes out with your question and your details, and you're in the copy. So you have their address, and they know you're coming.

04
You take it from there

They can simply hit reply. If you both want to, a call comes after. Three weeks on we ask you both whether it happened.

Are you an alumni yourself?

If you studied here and you're happy to hear from a student now and then, we'd love to add you. Send us your LinkedIn, and a phone number only if you want us to pass it on. We share nothing else, and you decide which topics you're up for.
